What actually happens on the call

  • 1.It answers on the first or second ring, in a South African accent, using your business name.
  • 2.It works out why the person is phoning: a new booking, a reschedule, a price question, or something that needs a human.
  • 3.If it is a booking, it offers real slots and takes the appointment there and then. No callback, no form.
  • 4.If it is not a booking, it takes the name, the number and the reason, and sends it to you so you start the day with a callback list instead of three voicemails.
  • 5.If it is urgent, it does not improvise. It points the caller at the right emergency route and flags the call to you immediately.

It does all of that at two in the morning on a Sunday exactly the way it does it at two in the afternoon on a Wednesday, and it handles ten calls at once as easily as one.

Against a traditional answering service

  • •Queueing. A human answering service shares its operators across every client it has. At half past eight in the morning you are in a queue. CTF answers every call at the same time, because there is no operator to wait for.
  • •Messages versus bookings. Most answering services take a message. CTF takes the booking, which is the part that actually earns you money.
  • •Per message pricing. Where a service charges per message, a busy month arrives as a bigger invoice. We charge a monthly fee based on volume, agreed up front.
  • •Consistency. It says the same thing on every call, at four in the morning, in December, on the two hundredth call of the week.

After hours answering in Johannesburg: questions

What is an after hours answering service?

It is a service that answers your business phone outside your trading hours, so a caller who rings at night, over a weekend or on a public holiday reaches a voice instead of voicemail. CTF does this with an AI receptionist rather than a human call centre agent, which is why it can answer every call at once and why it costs a fraction of a night shift.

Does CTF answer calls at night and over weekends in Johannesburg?

Yes. The line runs 24 hours a day, every day of the year, including public holidays. It does not queue, it does not take a lunch break and it answers the tenth simultaneous call the same way it answers the first.

Which Johannesburg areas do you cover?

All of them. Do you only serve Johannesburg?

No. CTF works anywhere in South Africa. A practice in Cape Town, Durban, Pretoria, Gqeberha or Bloemfontein is set up exactly the same way, on the same hours and at the same price. This page is written for Johannesburg because that is where we started, not because the service stops at the N1.

Will my callers know it is not a person?

Some will and some will not. We do not coach the receptionist to pretend to be human, and if a caller asks directly it says so. What matters more is that the call gets answered and the booking gets taken. Phone the demo line on this page and decide for yourself.

What happens to a call it cannot handle?

It takes the caller's name, number and reason for calling and sends it to you, so you have a callback instead of a missed call. For urgent clinical or legal matters it does not improvise. It directs the caller to the right emergency route and flags the call to you.

How is this different from a human answering service?

Human answering services typically charge per operator hour or per message, and their operators are shared across every client they have, so your caller can still end up in a queue at half past eight in the morning. CTF answers instantly, handles unlimited concurrent calls and books straight into a calendar rather than reading a message back to you the next day.

Do you replace my receptionist?

No. Most of our work sits in the hours your receptionist is not at the desk, plus the overflow when she is already on a call. The point is that the phone stops going unanswered, not that somebody loses a job.

What does it cost?

There is a once off build fee to configure and train the receptionist on your business, then a monthly fee based on your call volume and how many locations you run. We quote on a fifteen minute call once we know what your phone actually does.
