Three steps, from ringing phone to booked appointment.

STEP 01

Someone calls your business

Your existing number, or a dedicated CTF line, rings through — any time of day, on any day of the week.

// ABOUT US

Built to answer, when your business can't.

CTF started with a simple observation: South African businesses rarely lose customers because their service is bad — they lose them because the phone rang and nobody was there to answer it. We built an AI receptionist to close that gap, for businesses that can't justify a full-time front desk but can't afford to miss another booking either.

PILLAR 01

Built for South Africa

Local accents, local business hours, and an understanding of how South African customers actually talk on the phone — not a generic overseas call bot.

PILLAR 02

Every call answered

No voicemail, no "please hold," no missed calls after 5pm. If your phone rings, CTF picks up — every time, day or night.

PILLAR 03

Human backup, always

Anything the AI can't handle gets escalated straight to you. It's built to extend your team, not replace your judgment.
